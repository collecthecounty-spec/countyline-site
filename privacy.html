We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We share information only as needed to run the Site and business: with service providers (hosting, newsletter delivery, payment and order fulfillment) who need it to perform their function, and when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of CountyLine or others.

Cookies

The Site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember preferences and understand visitor activity. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings; doing so may affect how parts of the Site function.

Your Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we've collected about you, request its deletion, and opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" (as those terms are defined by law) — noting again that we do not sell personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information below.

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
