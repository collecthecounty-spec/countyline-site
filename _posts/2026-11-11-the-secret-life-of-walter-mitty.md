The Secret Life of a man constantly living in his head, imagining what could be, only to snap back into reality and carry on with his predictable life.

Walter's life is one many of us know. We stay on the safe path and stick to what we know. Except now, we don't even daydream. We live vicariously through scrolling and watching other people live the adventures for us.

### The Film

At least for me, no film will ever compare to the feeling or the tone that The Secret Life creates. I constantly find myself revisiting it time and time again, always leaving me with curiosity. The Secret Life is artistic, colorful, grounded in the mundane life but evolves from daydreaming into reality.

Setting the scene with music, cinematics, color theory, Stretch Armstrong, ghost cats, and a Sean O'Connell that is always out of reach. Finding Sean was Walter's pinnacle, the way finding the ghost cat was Sean's. Both on the hunt and going to extremes to find what it was that they were looking for. Only when Sean finally has the ghost cat in his lens, he doesn't take the shot, he just stays in the moment.

I have attempted to find something that makes me feel what The Secret Life made me feel. Whatever it was that Stiller and team put in this is irreplaceable. For that reason, this film has a special place in my heart. More than that, it resonates because I feel it parallels my life, except I haven't made the jump to my Nuuk, Greenland yet.

Doing what I need to do, but knowing that there is more out there. Doing the thing instead of thinking of doing the thing.

Like we all think of making the jump into the unknown or doing the thing we always thought about doing. We all have a feeling of hesitancy, anxiousness, and fear. We humans like predictability and comfort when we have it, yet we crave adventure and maintain curiosity of the unknown. The drive to find Negative 25 wasn't about getting it to his new boss, but was the catalyst into pushing him over the edge and bridging the gap of daydreams to Walter's new reality.

When he made the jump, the elaborate daydreams stopped. He started to live the life that he previously dreamt of. Most of us are still standing at the door of that helicopter. Somewhere between the life we wish for and the life we're afraid to leave behind.

— CL
